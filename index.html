Our story

A blue-and-white corner made for hanging out.

Suto Cafe sits just off Mantralayam Road, near IB Road, and has quickly become a regular stop for students, families and colleagues. The space is bright and a little playful — striped walls, round mirrors, balloons on the good days — built for sitting down properly rather than grabbing a quick cup.

Everything is made to order, service stays warm and attentive, and the space is kept genuinely clean and cared for — the details regulars bring up most often.
